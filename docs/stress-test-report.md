## Limits

- Generated events follow simple physics; real phones also slide, bounce in cup holders and get picked up after a crash.
- NHTSA records the car's floor, not a phone: a loose phone sees extra hits, a phone in a padded mount sees a softer pulse.
- MotionSense and the driving set have no GPS, so they exercise the no-GPS path, which is the strictest one.
- Thresholds are the team's experimental defaults (`CrashEvaluationConfiguration`), not crash-test validated.
- Crashes with a speed change under ~7 mph are deliberately not reported (`crashDeltaVMPS` = 3 m/s), like airbag systems: a phone can't tell a fender bender from a dropped bag.
- A 35 mph barrier crash peaks above the phone's sensor range, so the app's speed-change estimate reads low (~22 mph instead of ~40 at ±16 g). Detection doesn't depend on it.
