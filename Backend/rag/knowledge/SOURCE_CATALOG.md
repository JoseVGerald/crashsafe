## Active answer cards

### CALL_911 — What to tell the emergency call-taker

Tell the 911 call-taker your location, callback number, what happened, and the injuries or hazards you can observe. Answer their questions, follow their instructions, and stay on the line until told to hang up. This assistant does not place the call or confirm dispatch.

Scope: US; stage: any; sources: S01; expires: 2027-01-19.

### TEXT_911 — Calling versus texting 911

Call 911 by voice if you can. Text-to-911 availability varies; do not assume it works at your location. If you cannot speak, use an available local emergency text option and provide the location and type of emergency.

Scope: US; stage: any; sources: S02; expires: 2027-01-19.

### MOVEMENT — Possible neck back or head injury

With a possible head, neck or back injury, do not move the person simply to make them more comfortable. An immediate safety threat or essential lifesaving care can change what is necessary. Follow emergency-call instructions; do not use an automatic always-exit or always-stay rule.

Scope: US; stage: any; sources: S04, S05; expires: 2027-01-19.

### BLEEDING — Serious external bleeding

For life-threatening external bleeding, call 911 and follow emergency instructions. If it is safe to help, apply firm direct pressure to the wound. Do not remove an embedded object; pressure is applied around it. Advanced measures require appropriate training.

Scope: US; stage: any; sources: S06; expires: 2027-01-19.

### HEAD_INJURY — Head injury symptoms and danger signs

After a possible head injury, worsening headache, repeated vomiting, seizures, unusual confusion or difficulty waking require emergency medical care. Symptoms can appear later. Being able to talk does not establish that someone is uninjured.

Scope: US; stage: any; sources: S08; expires: 2027-01-19.

### FIRE — Vehicle on fire

If the vehicle catches fire, get out if you can, stay at least 100 feet away and away from traffic, and call 911. Do not return for belongings, fight the fire yourself or open a hood or trunk where you suspect fire.

Scope: US; stage: any; sources: S10; expires: 2027-01-19.

### EV — Electric and hybrid vehicle damage and towing

Damaged electric or hybrid high-voltage systems may remain energized, and fire or hazardous gases can occur later. Do not touch exposed electrical parts or attempt repairs. Tell responders and towing personnel the vehicle type. Follow the correct owner's manual for towing requirements.

Scope: US; stage: any; sources: S11; expires: 2027-01-19.

### CAR_SEAT — Car seat replacement after a collision

NHTSA recommends replacing car seats after moderate or severe crashes. A minor-crash exception requires all five conditions: vehicle driven away; nearest door undamaged; no occupant injuries; no deployed airbags; no visible seat damage. Always follow the seat manufacturer's instructions.

Scope: US; stage: any; sources: S12; expires: 2027-01-19.

### CALM_SUPPORT — Support when overwhelmed

A frightening event can leave people anxious, upset or unable to concentrate. Once immediate safety needs are addressed, focus on one manageable task and consider contacting someone you trust. You do not have to recount the event in detail to receive practical help.

Scope: US; stage: any; sources: S16, S13; expires: 2027-01-19.

### FOLLOWUP_EMOTION — When to seek emotional support after a crash

If distress does not improve, worsens or interferes with daily life, talk with a qualified health professional. Supportive friends or relatives and ordinary routines can help during recovery. This assistant does not diagnose PTSD or provide trauma therapy.

Scope: US; stage: followup; sources: S16; expires: 2027-01-19.

### WA_EXCHANGE — Washington required exchange of information

In Washington, collision duties include exchanging name, address, insurance company and policy number, vehicle license number, and showing a driver's license as required by RCW 46.52.020. The law also describes stopping, remaining at the appropriate location and providing reasonable assistance to injured people.

Scope: WA; stage: any; sources: S21; expires: 2027-01-19.

### WA_MOVE — Washington moving vehicles after property damage only

Washington's property-damage-only branch requires moving the vehicle from the roadway to a suitable location as soon as possible and remaining there to fulfill the required duties. The statute says moving the vehicle does not determine fault. Injury crashes have a separate branch; do not move an injured person just to reposition a car.

Scope: WA; stage: any; sources: S21; expires: 2027-01-19.

### WA_REPORT — Washington collision report conditions and deadline

For qualifying Washington collisions, a driver report is generally due within four days unless a law-enforcement report is to be made. WSP's portal identifies injury or at least $1,000 damage to any one unit, with an exception when the officer indicates they will file. Confirm that an officer will submit it; attendance alone is not that confirmation.

Scope: WA; stage: followup; sources: S22, S23; expires: 2027-01-19.

### DOCUMENT — Information and photographs for an insurance claim

Once it is safe, record the time, date, location, vehicle information, witness contacts and responding officer or report details. Photograph the scene and damage only from safe positions. Record what you actually observed and distinguish unknown details from estimates.

Scope: US; stage: any; sources: S29; expires: 2027-01-19.

### CLAIM_NOTICE — Notify your insurer and keep a claim record

Notify your insurer as soon as possible and cooperate with the claim investigation. Keep notes of conversations, dates and decisions. Ask for written explanations when you do not understand a claim decision.

Scope: US; stage: followup; sources: S29; expires: 2027-01-19.

### STATEMENT — Recorded statement and cooperation questions

Before answering a statement request, establish which insurer the person represents and check your own policy's cooperation requirements. A claims investigation may include a written or recorded statement. There is no universal rule to refuse every statement. If obligations or release terms are unclear, seek qualified advice.

Scope: US; stage: followup; sources: S29, S30; expires: 2027-01-19.

### TRUTHFUL — Accurate accounts and uncertain details

Describe what you actually saw and did. Do not invent a precise speed, guess at legal responsibility, hide relevant facts or exaggerate injuries. If you do not know a detail, say so; identify a genuine estimate as an estimate and correct mistakes when you discover them.

Scope: US; stage: any; sources: S29, S30; expires: 2027-01-19.

### COVERAGE — Coverage cannot be inferred from a generic guide

Check the actual policy and endorsements for coverage, exclusions, limits, deductibles and duties after a loss. A generic guide cannot establish that towing, a rental vehicle, repairs or medical costs are covered. Ask the insurer to identify the applicable terms in writing.

Scope: US; stage: followup; sources: S30; expires: 2027-01-19.

### DISPUTE — Disputed settlement or claim decision

You do not need to feel rushed into an insurance settlement you do not understand. Ask for a written explanation of the decision and review the relevant policy terms. If a claim-handling dispute remains unresolved, contact your state insurance department; legal questions may need a qualified lawyer.

Scope: US; stage: followup; sources: S29; expires: 2027-01-19.

### SILENCE — No response does not prove unconsciousness

No response from a phone user does not establish that the person is unconscious. Communication can fail. This prototype cannot determine consciousness or automatically dispatch emergency services; a real system needs separately validated detection and escalation procedures.

Scope: US; stage: any; sources: S01, S03; expires: 2027-01-19.

### CONCUSSION_RETURN — Driving and work after a concussion

After a suspected concussion, seek medical assessment. Ask your healthcare provider for written instructions about returning to driving, work and school. Tell them if symptoms worsen or are not improving; this assistant cannot clear you to drive.

Scope: US; stage: followup; sources: S36; expires: 2027-01-20.

### AIRBAG_REPLACE — Deployed air bags need replacement

Air bags work only once. Have deployed air bags replaced at an authorized repair center before driving again. Deployment can itself cause injuries; have medical concerns assessed rather than judging injury from vehicle appearance.

Scope: US; stage: any; sources: S37; expires: 2027-01-20.

### AIRBAG_LIMIT — Air bag deployment does not measure injury

Whether air bags deploy depends on the crash and vehicle system. Air bags may deploy in some relatively minor crashes and may not deploy in others. Their deployment alone cannot establish whether someone is injured or needs medical care.

Scope: US; stage: any; sources: S37; expires: 2027-01-20.

### INTERNAL_BLEED — Delayed or suspected internal bleeding

Internal bleeding may become apparent hours or days after an injury. Suspected internal bleeding, including blood in vomit or urine, a tender swollen or rigid abdomen, or signs of shock, needs emergency medical help. Call 911 and describe what you observe; do not wait for visible external bleeding.

Scope: US; stage: any; sources: S38; expires: 2027-01-20.

### CHEST_INJURY — Chest injury and breathing problems

After chest trauma, trouble breathing, severe pain or bruising, unusual chest movement, or coughing blood requires immediate emergency medical help. Call 911 and follow the call-taker's instructions. Do not assume these symptoms are anxiety.

Scope: US; stage: any; sources: S39; expires: 2027-01-20.

### ASK_SUPPORT — Ask a trusted person for concrete help

Once immediate safety needs are addressed, consider asking someone you trust for a specific kind of help, such as company or help with paperwork. You can choose whom to involve and explain what would help; support does not require telling everyone the full story.

Scope: US; stage: followup; sources: S40; expires: 2027-01-20.

### OPTIONAL_CALM — Calming exercises should be optional

In a safe setting after the emergency, relaxation may help some people but can initially increase distress for others. Choose a manageable approach and tell a clinician if it makes you feel worse. New physical symptoms after a crash still need medical assessment.

Scope: US; stage: followup; sources: S40; expires: 2027-01-20.

### GROUNDING — Present surroundings during a flashback

In a safe setting, if a memory feels as though it is happening again, keeping your eyes open and noticing your present surroundings may help orient you. Contact a trusted person or clinician for support. This is optional coping support, not a PTSD diagnosis or treatment.

Scope: US; stage: followup; sources: S40; expires: 2027-01-20.

### AVOID_SELF_MEDICATING — Alcohol and drugs are not a recovery plan

Using alcohol or drugs to escape traumatic memories or get to sleep can create further problems. If you are relying on them to cope, speak with a healthcare professional about support and safer treatment options. This assistant does not prescribe or change medication.

Scope: US; stage: followup; sources: S41; expires: 2027-01-20.

### CHILD_SUPPORT — Supporting a child after a crash

After addressing safety and medical needs, offer a calm, supportive environment and familiar routines. Let a child cry or express feelings in words or drawings. Do not force them to recount the crash, demand bravery, or promise a recovery timeline you cannot know.

Scope: US; stage: followup; sources: S20; expires: 2027-01-20.

### CHILD_FOLLOWUP — When a child needs more emotional support

Watch for new difficulties with sleep, school, behavior or withdrawal after a traumatic event. Contact the child's healthcare provider when new problems develop, especially when distressing reactions continue for weeks. Support is available without asking the app to diagnose the child.

Scope: US; stage: followup; sources: S20; expires: 2027-01-20.

### OR_REPORT — Oregon DMV collision report deadlines

In Oregon, file a DMV collision report within 72 hours for injury or death, damage to your vehicle over $2,500, damage to any vehicle over $2,500 with any vehicle towed, or nonvehicle property damage over $2,500. A police report does not replace this DMV report. If late, submit as soon as possible.

Scope: OR; stage: followup; sources: S42; expires: 2027-01-20.

### OR_FILE — Submitting an Oregon collision report

Oregon DMV2U online collision reporting requires that you were an involved driver and have an Oregon license, permit or ID number. Otherwise use the paper process. Save a copy of your submitted report; Oregon DMV cannot provide you a copy under the cited rule.

Scope: OR; stage: followup; sources: S42; expires: 2027-01-20.

### OR_SCENE — Oregon scene duties and police notification

In Oregon, stop and exchange name, address, driver-license number, plate and insurance information. Immediately notify police when DMV reporting criteria are met; use a nonemergency number for nonemergencies and 911 for emergencies. Do not carelessly move injured people.

Scope: OR; stage: any; sources: S42; expires: 2027-01-20.

### CA_REPORT — California SR-1 reporting deadline

In California, submit an SR-1 to DMV within 10 days when anyone is injured, however slightly, someone dies, or property damage exceeds $1,000. This report is separate from police, CHP and insurance reports. A driver, insurance agent, broker or legal representative may submit it.

Scope: CA; stage: followup; sources: S43; expires: 2027-01-20.

### CA_FILE — Information for a California SR-1

For California's SR-1, gather your driver-license or ID details, vehicle plate or VIN, insurance details and available information about the other party and vehicle. Use the official DMV process and keep your reporting obligations separate from your insurance claim.

Scope: CA; stage: followup; sources: S43; expires: 2027-01-20.

### WA_UNATTENDED — Washington collision with an unattended car

In Washington, if you strike an unattended vehicle, stop immediately. Locate and notify its owner or operator, or leave a conspicuous written notice in the struck vehicle with the names and addresses of both the driver and owner of the striking vehicle. Other reporting duties may also apply.

Scope: WA; stage: any; sources: S44; expires: 2027-01-20.

### REPAIR_ESTIMATE — Written repair estimates and authorization

Before authorizing repairs, ask for a signed written estimate identifying the repair, parts and labor charges. Ask that the shop obtain approval before exceeding an agreed amount. Ask about diagnostic fees and consider a second opinion for expensive or complicated work; specific legal rights vary by state.

Scope: US; stage: followup; sources: S45; expires: 2027-01-20.

### REPAIR_PARTS — Questions about replacement parts

Ask what replacement parts the shop proposes and what warranties cover parts and labor. New, remanufactured and salvage parts differ. A certification alone does not guarantee good work; ask about experience with your vehicle's make and model.

Scope: US; stage: followup; sources: S45; expires: 2027-01-20.

### COLLISION_COVERAGE — Collision versus liability coverage

Collision coverage generally addresses damage to your own car from a collision; liability coverage addresses covered harm you cause to others. Comprehensive coverage generally addresses other specified losses. Your policy, limits, deductibles and exclusions control the actual result; ask your insurer which coverage applies.

Scope: US; stage: followup; sources: S46; expires: 2027-01-20.

### GAP_LOAN — A total loss may leave an auto loan balance

A vehicle's value may be less than the loan balance. A vehicle-damage settlement may therefore leave money owed to the lender. Ask whether you purchased GAP coverage and what its terms cover; do not assume ordinary auto insurance pays the entire loan.

Scope: US; stage: followup; sources: S46; expires: 2027-01-20.

### UM_UIM — Uninsured and underinsured coverage questions

Ask your insurer whether you have applicable uninsured or underinsured motorist coverage when the other driver has no insurance or insufficient insurance. Hit-and-run eligibility and reporting requirements depend on the policy and state. Coverage is not established by the other driver's insurance status alone.

Scope: US; stage: followup; sources: S46; expires: 2027-01-20.

### RENTAL_TOW — Rental and towing coverage are policy dependent

Rental reimbursement and roadside or towing coverage are separate coverage questions. Ask your insurer whether you purchased them, what limits and conditions apply, and what documentation is needed before assuming a bill will be reimbursed.

Scope: US; stage: followup; sources: S46; expires: 2027-01-20.

### WA_PIP — Washington PIP is offered but can be rejected

Washington law requires PIP to be offered with new or renewed auto liability policies, subject to statutory exceptions. A named insured may reject it in writing, and that rejection may carry forward. Ask your insurer to confirm your coverage and any rejection; PIP is not automatically present on every policy.

Scope: WA; stage: followup; sources: S47; expires: 2027-01-20.

### WA_INSPECTION — Washington photo estimate disagreement

Under Washington's rule effective October 18, 2026, insurers cannot require a photo-only damage evaluation as a coverage condition. After virtual inspection, if there is a disagreement over the loss amount, they must accept a requested in-person inspection and inspect within five business days or another mutually agreed time.

Scope: WA; stage: followup; sources: S48; expires: 2027-01-20.

### WA_TOW_FEES — Washington written towing and storage coverage explanation

Washington's rule effective October 18, 2026 requires an insurer to give a first-party claimant a clear written explanation of applicable storage and towing coverage within five business days of claim notification. This is an explanation requirement, not a promise that every charge is covered.

Scope: WA; stage: followup; sources: S48; expires: 2027-01-20.

### WA_REPAIR_DIFFERENCE — Washington repair estimate differences

In Washington, when an insurer's repair amount differs from your repair estimate, ask for its reasons and relevant policy provisions. The current rule requires explanations for differences and consideration of additional crash-related damage. Choosing a more expensive repair facility may leave you responsible for amounts above the insurer's estimate.

Scope: WA; stage: followup; sources: S48; expires: 2027-01-20.
